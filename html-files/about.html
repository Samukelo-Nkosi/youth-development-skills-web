About Us

The Lathebe Skills Development Initiative is dedicated to empowering young people through education, innovation, and mentorship. We believe that every youth deserves the opportunity to build practical skills and achieve their full potential.

Since our founding in 2021, we have trained hundreds of youth in fields such as digital literacy, entrepreneurship, and vocational skills. Our programs aim to reduce unemployment and promote self-sustainability among young individuals.

Our Mission

To equip youth with essential skills that enhance employability, creativity, and innovation.

Our Vision

To build a generation of skilled, confident, and empowered youth ready to shape the future.

Our Values

  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Inclusivity
  • Commitment

A Message From Our Director

"When we started this initiative in 2021, our goal was simple: give young people the tools they need to build their own futures. Every student who walks through our doors reminds us why this work matters. Skills change lives — and confident, capable youth change communities."

— Executive Director, Lathebe Skills Development Initiative

Our Journey

2021

Lathebe Skills Development Initiative is founded with a single digital literacy pilot program.

2022

Expanded into vocational training, partnering with local trade schools.

2023

Launched the Entrepreneurship track and crossed 300 students trained.

2025

Reached 500+ students trained across 15 communities, with a 95% employment rate among graduates.
